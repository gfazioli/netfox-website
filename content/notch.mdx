# Notch HUD

On a MacBook with a notch, Netfox can put your network *right where your eyes already are*. The **Notch HUD** is a small overlay that grows out of the notch: idle, it's a discreet pill fused with the black cutout; reach for it and it expands into a live panel showing who's on your network, your risk level, your public IP and live traffic. No window, no menu bar trip — just glance up.

![The Netfox Notch HUD expanded under the notch](/screenshot-notch.png)

<Callout type="info">
The Notch HUD is only offered on Macs that actually have a notch (the 14″/16″ MacBook Pro and the MacBook Air from 2022 onward). On a Mac without a notch the feature — and its Settings pane — stay hidden.
</Callout>

## Turning it on

Open **Settings → Notch** and switch on **Show the Notch HUD**. A discreet pill appears just below the notch right away; everything else — the layout, how it opens, and which modules it carries — is configured from the same pane.

![The Notch pane in Settings](/screenshot-settings-notch.png)

## Choose a layout

The expanded HUD comes in **four layouts**, so you can match it to how much you want on screen. Pick one in **Settings → Notch → Layout**.

**Horizontal** — a single compact row of key figures spread to the sides of the notch. The lightest option.

![The Horizontal notch layout](/screenshot-notch-horizontal.png)

**Vertical** — a narrow column with one labelled row per module. Tidy and readable.

![The Vertical notch layout](/screenshot-notch-vertical.png)

**Panel** — a roomy grid of cards (the default). The most "dashboard at a glance".

![The Panel notch layout](/screenshot-notch-panel.png)

**Super Island** — the richest: a tabbed mini-dashboard under the notch, covered next.

## Super Island

**Super Island** turns the notch into a compact dashboard with four tabs:

- **Devices** — everything Netfox knows is on your network, online state at a glance
- **Risk** — only the devices currently flagged high or medium
- **Traffic** — the live throughput chart (see below)
- **Alerts** — what's currently in your alert inbox

Click any row and Netfox opens the main window **straight on that device's detail** — not just the Overview — so a name that catches your eye is one click from the full story.

![Super Island, with its Devices / Risk / Traffic / Alerts tabs](/screenshot-notch-super-island.png)

## How it opens

You decide what reveals the expanded HUD, in **Settings → Notch → Trigger**:

- **Hover** — the panel opens when you move the pointer onto the notch and closes when you leave
- **Click to pin** — a click pins the panel open so it stays put; click again to dismiss it
- **Hover + click to pin** — hover for a quick peek, click when you want it to stay

## What it shows

The same essentials you'd find on the [Overview](/docs/tools/overview) and in the [Menu Bar](/docs/menu-bar) popover, distilled to a glance:

- **Devices** — how many are online out of the total Netfox knows about
- **Risk** — your current risk level, tinted by severity
- **Public IP** — your network's outward address, with a VPN indicator
- **Traffic** — your Mac's live download and upload

Turn individual modules on or off in **Settings → Notch → Show**, so the HUD carries exactly what you care about and nothing else.

## Live traffic

The **Traffic** view charts what your Mac is moving on the network, sampled once a second over a rolling minute — download as a filled curve, upload as a line, with peak readouts and a faint grid for scale. It's the same live chart as the [Menu Bar](/docs/menu-bar) popover, sized for the notch.

![The Traffic view inside the Notch HUD](/screenshot-notch-traffic.png)

<Callout type="info">
This is your **Mac's** traffic — what this machine is downloading and uploading. Per-device bandwidth for the rest of the network is on the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap).
</Callout>

## Demo Mode

The Notch HUD respects **Demo Mode** just like the rest of the app. Flip it on with <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>D</Kbd> and the HUD masks your public IP, device names and the rest before you screenshot or screen-share. See [Settings → Privacy](/docs/settings#privacy) for the full mask reference.
