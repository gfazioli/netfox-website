# Menu Bar

Sometimes you don't want the whole app — you just want to know if your network is OK. The **menu bar** popover gives you that answer in a single click. Look for the small Netfox fox icon up in the macOS menu bar: click it and you get a compact, at-a-glance snapshot of your network without ever opening the main window.

![Netfox menu bar popover](/screenshot-menu-bar.png)

## What it shows at a glance

The popover lays out the essentials as cards on a subtle gradient, so you can read the state of your network in a second:

- **Devices** — how many are online right now, out of the total Netfox knows about
- **Risk level** — the headline posture, with the count of high- and medium-risk findings
- **Public IP** — your network's outward address, plus a chip telling you whether a VPN tunnel is active — with a one-click copy button once the address resolves
- **Alerts** — how many alerts are currently active in the inbox
- **Traffic** — a live chart of your Mac's network throughput over the last minute: download as a filled curve, upload as a line, with live readouts next to them

It's the same information you'd find on the [Overview](/docs/tools/overview), distilled into a glance-and-go panel. When something looks off — a risk level you didn't expect, an alert you haven't seen — one click takes you into the full app to dig in via the [Security](/docs/tools/security) or [Devices](/docs/tools/devices) tools.

## Live traffic

The **Traffic** card charts what your Mac is actually moving on the network, sampled once a second over a rolling minute. A download ramping up, a backup pulsing in the background, a video call holding steady — you can see the shape of it without opening anything.

The chart reads the Mac's own network counters, so it needs no special permissions and costs effectively nothing to keep running. Because sampling runs for the whole life of the app, the chart opens already filled with the last minute of history — no awkward empty graph while it warms up.

<Callout type="info">
This is your **Mac's** traffic — what this machine is downloading and uploading. Per-device bandwidth for everything else on the network is on the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap) and needs deeper system access, which Netfox will ask for explicitly when that ships.
</Callout>

## Pick your icon

The menu bar mark comes in two styles, and you choose in **Settings → General → Appearance**:

- **Monochrome** (default) — a fox-silhouette glyph that follows the macOS convention: it adapts automatically to light and dark menu bars and inverts correctly when the popover is open, just like the system's own status icons
- **Full Color** — the classic colour fox, if you prefer the brand mark over system adaptation

The switch applies instantly — no relaunch needed.

## Stays in your menu bar

Closing the main window no longer quits Netfox. Instead, the app stays resident in the menu bar, quietly keeping its picture of your network up to date. Discovery runs for the whole life of the app — not just while a window is open — so the popover always reflects the network as it is *right now*, even if you haven't opened the main window in days. The popover is always a click away.

When you want the full experience back, click **Open Netfox** in the popover — the main window reopens right where you left off.

<Callout type="info">
Netfox now lives in two places at once: the full window when you want depth, and the menu bar popover when you just want a quick check. Closing the window simply tucks the app away — it keeps running in the menu bar instead of shutting down.
</Callout>

## Quick actions

The footer of the popover keeps the things you reach for most one click away:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open Netfox** | Reopens the full app window |
| **Check for Updates** | Asks the updater whether a newer version is available |
| **Help** | Opens the documentation and help resources |
| **Settings** | Jumps straight to Netfox's preferences |
| **Sponsor** | Opens GitHub Sponsors, if you'd like to support development |

## Demo Mode

The menu bar respects **Demo Mode** just like the rest of the app. Flip it on with <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>D</Kbd> before you screenshot or screen-share, and the popover masks your public IP and VPN status alongside everything else — no real values leak into the recording. See [Settings → Privacy](/docs/settings#privacy) for the full mask reference.
