Netfox 0.6.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.6.0 is the redesign. The whole app moves to a dark, full-bleed gradient that shifts colour with every tool — so a glance tells you where you are — and the things you reach for most now work from anywhere.

🎨 A new look

Netfox is now dark by design, with a living gradient canvas: violet on the Overview, teal on Wi-Fi, blue on Devices, red on Security, green on Optimization. The sidebar floats as a translucent panel that picks up each tool's colour, the cards share one glass treatment, and the whole window reads as a single, modern surface instead of a stack of grey boxes.

🖱 Click straight through from the menu bar

The menu bar popover's cards are now live links. Click Devices, Risk, Public IP or Alerts and Netfox opens the main window — recreating it if you'd closed it — right on the matching tool. The popover also keeps its live traffic chart and one-click Public IP copy.

⌨️ Commands that work from any tool

Refresh (⌘R), Probe Ports (⌘⇧P) and Alert History (⌘⇧A) used to grey out unless you were on the Devices tool. They're now available everywhere — fire a refresh or open your alert history from Overview, Wi-Fi, Security, wherever you are.

✨ Polish throughout

  • The device list, the Wi-Fi network list and the Security views all wear the new design language, with smoother scrolling and cleaner panels.
  • The Devices search field sits where you expect it, pinned to the trailing edge of the toolbar.
  • Cards lift and glow on hover so it's obvious what's clickable.

🗺 Roadmap

The public roadmap now commits to the order of upcoming modules rather than pinning each to a version — Wi-Fi diagnostics is up next, then link diagnostics, then the per-device bandwidth monitor.
