Netfox 0.16.6

Release notes

A small correction to Local Services: the names from your hosts file now appear where they actually belong.

Local Services

  • A hosts name belongs to the address, not to each service. A single name pointing at 127.0.0.1 reaches every service listening there, so it was printed beside every one of them — true of all, and useful for telling apart none. Those names are now stated once, in the group heading: “On this Mac they also answer as localhost and shop.local.”
  • A name for one specific address stays on its row. That is the case where it earns its place: two listeners bound to two different addresses really do answer to two different names.
  • The heading says localhost too. Repeated on every row it was noise; said once, above the group, it tells you where you are.
  • Demo Mode keeps localhost and hides your own names. They come from your hosts file, they are as personal as a hostname, and this text lands in screenshots.

Fixes

  • The exposed group gets its own wording, because a service listening on one LAN address does not answer on loopback — claiming otherwise would have moved the same overstatement from the rows into the heading.
