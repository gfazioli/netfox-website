Netfox 0.51.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.51.0 lets the narrow panes fold away what you don't read, makes the notch's alerts say what happened, and keeps Netfox lean when it runs for days in the background.

Sections that fold

  • In a device's details, every section of the General tab folds: click its title to hide it. It stays hidden on every device and after a restart.
  • The Overview's Link card folds its Wired and Wireless sections the same way. A folded section shows an orange mark when what it hides carries a warning, such as a link below its top speed, errors or weak headroom, so folding never hides a problem.
  • A Wi-Fi network's details fold Network Information and Advanced.
  • Every section starts open, and VoiceOver says whether each one is open or folded, in every language.

Notch

  • Each alert in the notch's dashboard now says what happened, such as a new device, one that's back or a port that opened, instead of only naming the device. A risky new device is marked in red.
  • The header names the tab you're on, each tab says its name when you point at it, and the bell carries a badge while there are alerts.

Long runs

  • Netfox no longer grows in memory when it runs for days with the window closed. Alerts older than 90 days leave the in-app lists, as they already left the history on disk, and the history drops them at launch too.
  • A Wi-Fi network out of range for ten minutes starts a fresh signal line when it comes back, instead of one joined to samples from hours before.
  • An unread alert older than 90 days no longer counts on the bell.

Fixes

  • The Overview's cards no longer play their entrance again while you resize the window, and an open card stays open as the cards rearrange.
  • The Discord logo in the About window is drawn whole again.
