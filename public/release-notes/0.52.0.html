Netfox 0.52.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.52.0 notices a connection that stalls: the Overview's Link card no longer calls a router fine just because its fastest reply was fast.

Link

  • The Link card now catches a connection that answers quickly and then stops. When the router replies in a few milliseconds but a share of the pings comes back more than 100 ms late, the card says the first hop is stalling and gives the share, where before it called the router fine. It waits for two rounds of pings before judging, so one late reply never reads as a stall.
  • When the router answers steadily and only the path past it stalls, the card says the delay is past the router, not on your own link.
  • One lost ping among twenty no longer marks the router as flaky: loss now counts from one ping in ten.

Fixes

  • If one of the ways Netfox looks for devices cannot start, the others now keep running instead of discovery stopping altogether.
