Netfox 0.51.1

Release notes

Netfox 0.51.1 is a small patch: the cards on the Overview and the Security page open and close without starting over, the About window looks right in every language, and VoiceOver reads risk levels the way they are meant to be read.

Cards

  • Opening or closing a card on the Overview or the Security page keeps what is inside it instead of building it again.
  • On the Overview, a card you open or close during a refresh no longer gives its small press a moment after the others.

About

  • The cards of our other apps in the About window now stand level in every language. In German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese one of them stood taller than the others.
  • An app's description is never cut short: in Spanish and Portuguese, FinderGit's lost its last words.

VoiceOver

  • A port with a security finding reads "Medium risk finding" instead of "Medium risk risk finding".
  • A device at risk, in the Overview's Risk card or on the Security page, says its risk level once, in every language.
  • In Security's By Service view, the number of devices at risk is spoken in your language instead of in English.
