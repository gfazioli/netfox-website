Netfox 0.24.0

Release notes

Netfox's whole release history is now readable inside the app.

Release Notes, in the app

  • Help → Release Notes… opens the whole release history, newest first, offline and in your language. Until now the panel after an update showed one release, and the Help menu could only reopen that same one; the rest were already inside the app, translated, and reachable only on the website.
  • A dot marks what arrived since you last opened the window, so after a few quiet updates you can see at a glance what you missed.
  • Releases from before Netfox was translated say "English only" rather than switching language on you without warning.

About

  • Lancetta joins the apps in About — a menu-bar monitor for coding agents. The About panel shows itself once when that list changes, so after updating it may turn up on one launch you did not ask it to; the checkbox at its foot decides whether it opens at launch at all.
