Netfox 0.25.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.25.0 is about feel: pull a tool down to refresh it, and watch the Overview and the menu bar panel fill in.

Pull to refresh (macOS 27)

  • Pull a view down to refresh it, as in Mail. The Overview reads again what Netfox reads at launch: the devices, the public IP and the VPN. Wi-Fi scans for networks right away, Devices refreshes the list, and Security runs the same scan as its blue button.
  • The spinner stays for a moment and then lets go, and the refresh carries on in the background. A scan that is already running is never started twice. The sidebar doesn't refresh: it is for choosing a tool.

Glass (macOS 26 and later)

  • The Overview's cards are glass, each in the colour of the tool it opens. They keep that colour, and stay just as readable, when Netfox is behind another app.
  • The menu bar panel has glass cards over a softly lit backdrop, a violet Open Netfox button and round glass buttons.

Motion

  • The rings fill and the figures roll into place, the public IP included, when the Overview or the panel appears, and again on every refresh. With Reduce Motion on, nothing moves.

Fixes

  • In the menu bar panel, the Public IP card sat a few points lower than the Alerts card beside it. The two now line up.
