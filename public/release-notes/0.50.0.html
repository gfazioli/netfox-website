Netfox 0.50.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.50.0 makes the Overview and the Security page open in place, and keeps watching your network when the window is closed.

Overview

  • The Risk, Devices and Alerts cards open in place: a click shows the devices at risk with what was found on each, the devices online, or the latest alerts. The ↗ arrow in a card's corner takes you to the tool, and every row opens its device, on the Ports tab for a device at risk or an alert about a port.
  • The Overview opens on what needs attention: the Risk card when a device is at risk, otherwise the Alerts card when there are alerts.

Security

  • The counts by severity are now four cards that open in place, the most serious one already open when you arrive. Low-risk devices finally have a list of their own.

Always watching

  • Closing the main window no longer stops discovery, alerts, scheduled port scans or the metrics endpoint: Netfox keeps watching from the menu bar until you quit.

Privacy

  • Settings → Privacy now names everything the public IP switch stops: the lookup itself and the Link card's check of the route out.
  • Forget all devices also wipes the Wi-Fi samples, and every wipe in Privacy works with the main window closed.
  • The automatic update switch shows the setting the updater really uses, and the wipe confirmations are translated.

Fixes

  • A USB Ethernet adapter on the driver built into macOS no longer gets a "below its top speed" warning: its top speed can't be read, and the Link card says so.
  • This Mac no longer shows 127.0.0.1 as its address.
  • A device first seen only by its address no longer leaves a stored row that later sessions can't match.

Small things

  • The About window is laid out in two columns that fit a 13-inch screen in every language, with icons on its links and a new link to the community on Discord.
  • In Demo Mode, This Mac's ports show invented processes, so a screenshot shows who holds a port without showing what runs on your Mac.
