Netfox 0.55.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.55.0 dresses up for the holidays: from 1 December to 6 January the fox wears a Santa hat, and the Overview gets snow and a string of lights that follows your network.

In season

  • From 1 December to 6 January, by your Mac's own date, the fox wears a Santa hat wherever Netfox shows its icon: the Overview, the Devices and Wi-Fi headers, the menu bar popover, About, Settings, the Dock while Netfox runs, and the Full Color menu bar icon.
  • On the Overview, snow falls and settles on the summary cards, building up over a minute and a half, and the waves behind them turn into an aurora.
  • A string of lights hangs across the top: one bulb for every device on your network, lit while it is online. The lights twinkle faster while your Mac is busy on the network.
  • The decorations arrive on 1 December and leave on 7 January by themselves, with no update needed and even with Netfox left running. The snow and the lights pause while the window is hidden, minimised or on another Space.
  • Prefer Netfox plain? Settings → General → Appearance → Festive decorations turns it all off; the switch is shown only during the season. With Reduce Motion on, no snow falls and the lights hold still.

Demo Mode

  • On 24 and 25 December, with sample data on, Santa's sleigh passes through the Devices list. It is the one invented row, and nothing else sees it: no count, alert or history.
