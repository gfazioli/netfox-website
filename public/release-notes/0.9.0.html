Netfox 0.9.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.9.0 brings the Notch HUD — your network, live, right out of your MacBook's notch — plus sharper device recognition across the board.

Notch HUD

  • A Dynamic-Island-style overlay grows out of the notch with live device, risk, public-IP and traffic readouts. Idle it's a discreet pill; reach for it and it expands.
  • Four layouts to match how much you want on screen: Horizontal, Vertical, Panel, and the rich Super Island — a tabbed dashboard (Devices · Risk · Traffic · Alerts) where clicking a row jumps straight to that device's detail.
  • Open it your way: on hover, on click to pin it open, or hover-then-click.
  • A live traffic chart sized for the notch, with a steadier scale and min/max guides.
  • Off by default, and only offered on Macs that actually have a notch. Turn it on in Settings → Notch.

Sharper device recognition

  • Your gateway is now labelled as a Router.
  • Netfox reads the product behind a device's web page and flags pages that require a login.
  • Recognizes more vendors and ports out of the box.

Fixes & polish

  • A steadier device list — brief network blips no longer make devices flicker in and out.
  • The colored menu bar icon is now the default (switch to monochrome in Settings → General).
  • Settings: About now lives in the sidebar header ("About Netfox").
