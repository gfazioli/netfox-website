Netfox 0.10.0

Release notes

Netfox 0.10 is the readability release. It works harder to tell you what each device is — in plain English — instead of leaving you with hex addresses and port numbers.

Know what's on your network

  • Plain-English identity — a device with no friendly name now leads with what it is — "Netgear · Router · Web service" — instead of a bare IP address. The address drops to the subtitle, where it belongs. Devices you've named, or that report a hostname, keep that name up front as before.
  • More services recognised — more of what a device announces on the network now feeds its identity, so more devices read as what they are at a glance instead of an anonymous address.

See what's actually running — the Service inspector

  • Service inspector — open a device's General tab and Netfox names the product behind its open ports: a router's SSH shell, an HP printer, a NAS, a smart-home web page. A bare "port 8080 open" becomes "that's the printer's web admin."
  • It reads only what a service announces about itself on connect — no login attempt, nothing intrusive — and stays off until you switch on Look up service info on open ports in Settings → Privacy.

Settings

  • A clearer reset — in Settings → Privacy, the full reset ("Forget all devices," which wipes everything) now sits in its own Reset everything section, clearly apart from the targeted history clears, so it can't be mistaken for one of them.

Netfox stays native, on-device, and account-free — everything it learns about your network lives only on your Mac. Requires macOS 15.6 or later.
