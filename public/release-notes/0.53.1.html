Netfox 0.53.1

Release notes

Netfox 0.53.1 clears what it keeps about your network when its time is up, as its privacy policy says, and says more precisely what two Settings switches do.

Privacy

  • What Netfox keeps now expires on time. Device history older than 30 days and Wi-Fi and latency readings older than a week are cleared when Netfox starts, not only when something new arrives. A quiet network, or a Mac that stopped scanning Wi-Fi, no longer keeps old records past their time.
  • The Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy are now linked from the Help menu and the About Netfox window.

Settings

  • Demo Mode's help lists everything it hides, Wi-Fi network names and IPv6 addresses included.
  • The help for Notify when a new service is identified points to the switch it depends on, Look up service info on open ports, in Privacy.
  • In German, Spanish and French, the Privacy pane now carries its name in your language.
